## Manuscript explanatory-layer finding — 2026-09-30 12:55 +08:00

- Problem: `sensitivity to mapping and scoring choices` is semantically compressed and does not provide enough explanatory bridge for the reader to connect the general gene-set-analysis limitation to the AIDO-h observation-readiness problem.
- `mapping` may ambiguously mean gene-ID mapping, probe-to-gene mapping, gene-to-pathway mapping, database annotation, or cross-platform mapping. The manuscript should state the intended layer explicitly.
- For this study, the key chain should be made visible: gene-set definition → actual matched gene/feature representation in the dataset → pathway-score construction → endpoint-specific discriminability.
- Candidate clarification: `sensitivity to gene-to-pathway mapping, feature coverage, and pathway-scoring choices`, followed by one explanatory layer showing why incomplete/different matched representation and different scoring methods can alter pathway-level observables and downstream discrimination.
- This is not merely stylistic polishing; it is a rationale-bridging issue. Add an explanatory layer during the batch manuscript repair rather than relying on readers to infer the connection.
